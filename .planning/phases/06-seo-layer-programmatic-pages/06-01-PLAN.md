<assumption_delta_decision>
The hreflang identity model changes from "identical DE/EN paths" (Phase 2 `buildHreflangAlternates(path)`) to "differing per-locale slugs" (D-03/D-04). The now-primary noun is the counterpart-locale-resolved slug pair (resolved via `translation.metadata`, D-04). Decision: **promote** the paired-slug hreflang helper (`buildHreflangAlternatesPaired`) as the primary path for SEO pages, while the shared `buildHreflangAlternates(path)` **stays** for identical-path routes (home, sections, legal). Add-alongside is acceptable here because the two helpers serve genuinely different route shapes — identical-path routes must NOT be migrated to the paired variant. Advisory, non-blocking.
</assumption_delta_decision>
