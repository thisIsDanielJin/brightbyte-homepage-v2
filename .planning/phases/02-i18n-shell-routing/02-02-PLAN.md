<user_story>
**As a** search engine crawling brightbyte.berlin,
**I want to** find correct bidirectional hreflang on every URL and in the sitemap, with `x-default` pointing to the German root,
**so that** German and English visitors are each served the right locale in search results and the German-first site is indexed cleanly.
</user_story>

Built as the reusable seam Phase 6's ~30 programmatic pages extend without a rewrite (D-07, D-08).

Purpose: I18N-02 is the SEO backbone of the bilingual site — the seam every later page and the Phase 6 SEO layer depend on. Building it now, once, as a shared helper prevents per-page hreflang drift.

Output: `lib/i18n/metadata.ts` helper, `[locale]/page.tsx` generateMetadata wired to it, and `app/sitemap.ts` — with smoke tests (4)-(5) flipping GREEN.
</objective>
